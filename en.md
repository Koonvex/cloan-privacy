# CLoan+ Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-17

Maker: Koonvex (independent studio) — contact: koonvex@gmail.com

## 1. Summary

CLoan+ is free to use and does not require an account. The loan scenarios you create are stored on your device. We do not sell your data and the app does not show ads.

## 2. Data stored on your device

Your saved scenarios (loan amounts, rates, dates, notes you type) are stored in a local database in the app. We cannot see them, and the app is set so that Android's built-in device backup does not copy this database to your Google account.

If you use "Back up" the app writes a file to a location you choose; if you use "Share" the app creates an image and passes it to the app you choose. Those actions only happen when you tap them.

## 3. Data collected by the app's services

The app uses two Google Firebase services:

- **Firebase Crashlytics** collects crash reports so we can find and fix bugs. A report may include the error and stack trace, device model, Android version, app version, whether the app was in the foreground, and an installation identifier generated by Firebase. Reports do not include your loan scenarios, and crash collection is disabled in developer builds.
- **Firebase Remote Config** downloads small settings from our Firebase project (for example a maintenance notice or a minimum required app version). Doing so sends your device's IP address and an installation identifier to Google, as with any network request.

IP addresses and installation identifiers may count as personal data under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), so we treat them accordingly. Google processes this data as described in the Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 4. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifier. We do not use the app for behavioral analytics or tracking.

## 5. How we use the data

Crash reports are used only to diagnose and fix bugs. Remote Config values are used only to operate the app (maintenance and update notices).

## 6. Retention

Crash data is kept by Firebase for the period set by Google. Scenarios on your device are kept until you delete them or uninstall the app.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can delete any scenario in the app, or uninstall the app to remove local data. Under the PDPA you have rights such as access, correction, deletion and objection regarding your personal data. To exercise them or to ask about crash data tied to your device, email koonvex@gmail.com with your app version and device model; because reports are not linked to your identity, we may not be able to find a specific report.

## 8. Children

The app is a financial calculator for adults and is not directed to children.

## 9. Estimates

Results are estimates. Your bank's official quote always prevails.

## 10. Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change (for example if ads are added) and change the effective date.
